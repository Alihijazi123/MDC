Drilling and Equipping Water Wells

Page 3 of 11

The execution follows a sequence of work phases that ensures proper completion:

1
Site Assessment: Check Hydro-geological Info System, field survey, legal aspects, formal ministry approval.
2
Locating Underground Water: Examine site and maps, use specialized tools to locate basin.
3
Rig Setup: Clean workspace, level drilling machine (rotary/hammer), setup drainage pits.
4
Hole Drilling: Initiate drilling works until water is found.
5
Well Casing: Insert metal/PVC/stainless steel pipes to let water in and block dirt.
6
Grouting: Seal space outside pipe with clay or cement.
7
Pump Setup: Select submersible or surface pump based on volume/depth tests.
8
Wellhead Cap Setup: Install concrete aprons and secure caps to block contamination.
9
Power Systems Setup: Install grid, diesel generator, and/or solar panels.
10
Control and Automation Setup: Water sensors, float switches, digital timers, dry-run protection.
11
Water Filters Setup: Install filters for cleaning well water if need be.
12
Final Testing: Check mechanical and electrical systems to validate standards.

Installing Solar Power Systems for Well Pumps

Page 4 of 11

After selecting and installing the right water pump, the works follow this sequence:

  1. Selecting a suitable place: easily accessible, unbarred, year-round strong sunlight, allows future expansion.
  2. Installing the metal mounting structure.
  3. Mounting the solar panels and anchoring them properly against weather factors.
  4. Installing control panel, inverter, AC Output Reactor, Batteries, and protection devices.
  5. Wiring the system.
  6. Making the final tests and fine-tuning.
We ventured into this type of renewable energy mainly because it is eco-friendly, it reduces the client's operating costs, and it works well in remote locations.
